## Introduction
This case study revolves around my role as a junior data analyst for Bellabeat. The analysis follows the data process framework: Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, and Act.

## Background
Bellabeat is a women-owned smart device company founded in 2013 that focuses on empowering women's wellness and health. Urška Sršen and Sando Mur are the cofounders of this company, while also having a marketing analytics team which are responsible for the collection, analysis, and reporting of the data to support the marketing strategy. Sršen used her art background to create the iconic leaf-shaped smart device that can be worn as a necklace, clip-on or bracelet.

Bellabeat has launched multiple products and opened many offices globally while making their products available on multiple online retail sites. They also have their own ecommerce platform while advertising their product with various media, including billboards, radios, television and digital marketing. They continually invest in year-round Google search and maintain multiple social media pages, such as Instagram and Facebook, to keep up with the consumer interface.

The products Bellabeat currently offer includes the Bellabeat app, Leaf and Time. The Bellabeat app connects to their line of smart devices to provide users with health data to understand their current habits, including their sleep, activity, stress, mindful habits and menstrual cycle. The Leaf is a wellness tracker, Bellabeat's most well-known product which can be worn interchangeably as a bracelet, clip or necklace. Lastly, the Time Watch is a smart-watch product that tracks stress, sleep and activity, which can also connect to the Bellabeat app to gain more detailed insights about one's wellness. 


## Project Workflow

### 1. Project Objectives (Ask)

**Business task:** Analyze the Fitbit Watch dataset to observe how consumers use its different functionalities, including sleep rate, weight tracking, steps and activity intensities. Observing the most to the least used functionalities will allow us to uncover trends that can support the Bellabeat marketing strategy.

The three questions that will guide the future marketing program include:  

a. What are some trends in smart device usage?  

b. How could these trends apply to Bellabeat customers?  

c. How could these trends help influence Bellabeat's marketing strategy? 

### 2. Data Source (Prepare)

I will be using the [FitBit Fitness Tracker Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit) to address potential marketing strategies, specifically for the Leaf tracker. This dataset is under the [CC0: Public Domain license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This data was generated by Amazon Mechanical Turk, a crowdsourcing marketplace that connects businesses with consumer human intelligence. In this case, they did a survey involving 30 eligible Fitbit users who consented to the submission of their health tracking data. This data is credible since it is from a legitimate source being Amazon. I believe this data does not have any bias since the respondents were randomly chosen and their identities were not disclosed.

This dataset did have some limitations, which include:

a. Having a smaller sample size with only 30 Fitbit users.

b. Not having a clear demographic since no age, gender or sex is mentioned.

c. A limited timeframe since this data only ranges over 2 months from 03/16 - 05/16.

d. Limited data for certain features, e.g. the heart rate dataset, which was tracked by only 13 users. 


Tools for analysis:

a. **SQL** - for data processing, cleaning and exploration.

b. **Tableau** - for data visualization.

#### Data Merging

This dataset was a combination of the daily activity datasets, resulting in 1397 rows in total. The first one ranged from  March 12, 2016, to April 11, 2016, and the second one ranged from April 12, 2016, to May 12, 2016.

The data dictionary below will provide additional context regarding the 13 variables in the dataset.

| Variable          | Type    | Description                                 |
|-------------------|---------|---------------------------------------------|
| user_id           | Integer | Unique ID number for Fitbit users.          |
| total_steps       | Integer | Total number of steps taken.                |
| act_date          | Date    | Date in mm/dd/yyy format.                   |
| total_distance    | Float   | Total kilometers tracked.                   |
| very_act_dist     | Float   | Kilometres during very active activity.     |
| moderate_act_dist | Float   | Kilometres during moderate activity.        |
| light_act_dist    | Float   | Kilometres during light activity.           |
| sed_act_dist      | Float   | Kilometres during sedentary activity.       |
| very_act_min      | Integer | Total minutes in very active activity.      |
| fairly_act_min    | Integer | Total minutes in fairly active activity.    |
| lightly_act_min   | Integer | Total minutes in lightly active activity.   |
| sed_min           | Integer | Total minutes in sedentary activity. |
| total_calories    | Integer | Total calories lost.                        |

### 6. Final Recommendations (Act)

**Timed Engagement**
- Alerts and reminders should be present for both low and high engagement periods. For low-engagement periods, there should be gentle reminders, whereas for high-engagement periods, alerts should be insight-based to encourage users to engage more with the steps feature. This will encourage consistent movement and long-term engagement with the Bellabeat Leaf product.  

**Seasonal Campaigns**
- When observing the visuals, there was more engagement with the Fitbit watch from April to May. Bellabeat could focus on creating seasonal campaigns during the spring with special deals for in-app purchases and a discount on the Leaf product.

**Social Media Engagement**
- Social media is an integral part of how individuals consume media. Using Bellabeats' current social media pages, there should be constant engagement using short-form videos about how people incorporate the Leaf into their everyday lives. The benefits it offers in everyday life use could resonate with users, encouraging higher engagement. 

**Personalization Features**
- When looking at the activity levels feature, we can use this to tailor insights for users based on activity changes and adjust recommendations based on activity levels. This would be more encouraging for users since these insights would be personal to them.
