## Background
Bellabeat is a women-owned smart device company founded in 2013 that focuses on empowering women's wellness and health. Urška Sršen and Sando Mur are the cofounders of this company while also having a marketing analytics team which are responsible for the collection, analyzing and reporting of the data to support the marketing strategy. Sršen used her art background to create the iconic leaf-shaped smart device that can be worn as a necklace or bracelet.

Bellabeat has launched multiple products and opened many offices globally while making their products available on multiple online retail sites. They also have their own ecommerce platform while advertising their product with various media including billboards, radios, television and digital marketing. They continually invest in year-round google search and maintain multiple social media pages such as instagram and facebook to keep up with the consumer interface.

The products Bellabeat currently offer includes the Bellabeat app, Leaf and Time. The Bellabeat app connects to their line of smart devices to provide users with health data to understand their current habits including their sleep, activity, stress mindful habits and menstrual cycle. The Leaf, a wellness tracker is Bellabeats most well known product which can be worn interchangeably as a bracelet, clip or necklace. Lastly, the Time watch is a smart watch product that tracks stress, sleep and activity which can also connect to the bellabeat app to gain more detailed insights about one's wellness. 


## Project Workflow

### 1. Project Objectives (Ask)

**Business task:** Analyze the Fitbit Watch dataset to observe how consumers use its different functionalities including sleep rate, weight tracking, steps and activity intensities. Observing the most to the least use functionalities will allow us to uncover trends that can support the bellabeat marketing strategy.

The three questions that will guide the future marketing program includes:  

a. What are some trends in smart device usage?  

b. How could these trends apply to Bellabeat customers?  

c. How could these trends help influence Bellabeat marketing strategy? 

### 2. Data Source (Prepare)

I will be using the [FitBit Fitness Tracker Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit) to address potential marketing strategies, specifically for the Leaf tracker. This dataset is under the [CC0: Public Domain license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This data was generated by Amazon Mechanical Turk, a crowd sourcing marketplace that connects businesses with consumer human intelligence. In this case, they did a survey involving 30 eligible fitbit users that consented to the submission of their health tracking data. This data is credible since it is from a legitimate source being Amazon. I believe this data does not have bias since the respondents were randomly chosen and their identities are not disclosed.

This dataset did have some limitations which includes:

a. Having a smaller sample size with only 30 fitbit users.

b. Not having a clear demographic since no age, gender or sex is mentioned.

c. A limited timeframe since this data only ranges over 2 months from 03/16 - 05/16.

d. Limited data for certain features e.g. the heart rate dataset which only 13 users tracked. 


Tools for analysis:

a. **SQL** - for data processing, cleaning and exploration.

b. **Tableau** - for data visualization.
